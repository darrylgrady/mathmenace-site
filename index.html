Number puzzle game

MathMenace

A word search for numbers: find the equations hidden in the grid.

How to play

  • Pick a grid size (4 × 4 up to 12 × 12) and start.
  • Equations are hidden across, down and diagonally, forwards or backwards. Only the numbers show: the +, −, × and ÷ are left out, so 3, 4, 12 is 3 × 4 = 12.
  • Drag across the numbers of an equation to find it. Found tiles turn gray. Some equations have four numbers.
  • Hint lights the first two numbers of an equation you haven't found. Give up shows every equation that's left.
  • Your game is saved as you play, so you can leave and pick it up again.
