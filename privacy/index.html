MathMenace · number puzzle game

Privacy policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

In short: MathMenace doesn't collect, send or share any information about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. The puzzle you're in the middle of is kept on your own device so you can pick it up again, and nowhere else.

What the app keeps, and where

MathMenace saves the puzzle you're playing (its numbers, which equations you've found, and how long you've played) so that you can leave and come back to it. It keeps nothing else: no scores, no settings, nothing about you. The save stays on your device:

  • Android app: in the app's private storage on your phone. If you have Android's device backup turned on, Android may include it in your phone's backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; the game's developer can't see it.
  • iPhone app: in the app's private storage, which iOS may include in your iCloud or computer backups under your Apple account settings.
  • Browser version: in your browser's local storage for the game's page.

The save is deleted when you finish the puzzle, give up, or start a new one. Uninstalling the app deletes it too.

What the app doesn't do

  • It doesn't ask for or collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • It doesn't use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking services, and it doesn't share or sell anything.
  • The phone apps don't connect to the internet at all: the game is entirely inside the app.
  • It asks for no permissions beyond what every app has.

Children

MathMenace collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example by adding an online feature, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
